Process

How a project runs

No mystery, no scope creep. You always know what happens next and what it costs.

  1. 01

    Call & scope

    A 30-minute call to understand the business, the goal, and what success looks like. You get a written scope and a fixed price within two days.

  2. 02

    Design

    Wireframes first, then a full design of the key pages. We iterate here, where changes are cheap, before a line of code is written.

  3. 03

    Build

    I build against a live preview link you can check any time. Weekly updates, never three weeks of silence.

  4. 04

    Launch & handover

    Deploy, analytics, search console, and a walkthrough video of how to edit your own content. Thirty days of free fixes after launch.

FAQ

Questions clients ask

How long does a project take?

A landing page is about a week. A business site is two to four weeks. Apps and stores depend on scope, but you get a date in writing before we start, and I hit it.

Do I own the code and the site?

Yes. Everything lives in your accounts and your repository. No proprietary builder, no hostage situation if you decide to move on.

Can I edit content myself afterwards?

That is the default. Business sites ship with a CMS, and I record a short walkthrough showing how to change text, images, and pages.

What does hosting cost?

Most sites I build run on free or near-free hosting. Expect $0–$20 a month depending on traffic, plus roughly $12 a year for the domain. You pay those providers directly, never me.

Do you redesign existing sites?

Often. I audit what you have first and tell you honestly whether a rebuild is worth it or whether a few targeted fixes would do more for less.

What happens after launch?

Thirty days of free bug fixes. After that, either ad-hoc work at an hourly rate or a small monthly retainer covering updates, backups, and monitoring.
